## About me

I'm a Product Manager and builder with **9 years of experience** across AI, product strategy, and execution. I previously worked at **EdgeTensor** and **Quantiphi** and am currently building **[Graph Contract](https://graphcontract.dev)**.

I enjoy turning ambitious ideas into clear product systems: understanding the customer problem, defining the right workflow, aligning people and technology, and shipping something genuinely useful.

## Graph Contract

I'm currently building **[Graph Contract](https://graphcontract.dev)**, a human-controlled visual workspace for designing agent workflows. People edit and approve the graph while external agents inspect the accepted contract and propose structured changes through WebMCP.

The goal is to help teams build reliable agent workflows where automation can move quickly without taking decision-making control away from people.

## What I'm building

- Autonomous agent systems that can plan, build, test, deploy, and improve software
- Human-in-the-loop products where AI can move quickly while people retain final control
