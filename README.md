## About me

I'm a Product Manager and builder with **9 years of experience** across AI, product strategy, and execution. I previously worked at **EdgeTensor** and **Quantiphi** and am currently building **[Manki Studios](https://mankistudios.com)** and **[GraphContract](https://graphcontract.dev)**.

I enjoy turning ambitious ideas into clear product systems: understanding the customer problem, defining the right workflow, aligning people and technology, and shipping something genuinely useful.

## Manki Studios

I'm currently building **[Manki Studios](https://mankistudios.com)**, an AI UGC ad-creation product that helps teams create short-form video ads using 100+ AI actors, AI-assisted scripts, previews, and downloadable creative.

The goal is to make high-volume ad production faster and more accessible while keeping people in control of the creative decisions.

## GraphContract

I'm currently building **[GraphContract](https://graphcontract.dev)**, a human-controlled visual workspace for designing agent workflows. People edit and approve the graph while external agents inspect the accepted contract and propose structured changes through WebMCP.

The goal is to help teams build reliable agent workflows where automation can move quickly without taking decision-making control away from people.

## What I'm building

- An India-first AI creative engine for producing high-volume, performance-oriented video ads
- Autonomous agent systems that can plan, build, test, deploy, and improve software
- Human-in-the-loop products where AI can move quickly while people retain final control

## Product principles

```text
customer pain → clear system → fast feedback → reliable execution
```

I care about products that are useful before they are impressive: clear ownership, measurable outcomes, thoughtful automation, and fast learning from real users.
